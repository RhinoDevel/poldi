From the dialog, extract facts about the user or the user's close relations (family, friends, pets).

Rules:

- Focus only on stable facts: identity, preferences, and close relations.
- Do not change or upgrade relationship types or strength (e.g. cousin must remain cousin, acquaintance must not become friend).
- Do not include questions from the user.
- Do not include replies or behavior of the assistant.
- Do not describe the conversation itself.
- Facts must describe stable properties of the user, not one-time actions or temporary activities.
- Do not include projects, tasks, plans, or activities the user is doing or planning.
- Ignore single events or temporary situations.
- Prefer facts that are generally true over time.
- Each fact must be understandable without referring to other facts.
- Each fact must be fully self-contained and must not assume any entity introduced in other lines. If necessary, repeat context within the same line.
- Use neutral, timeless phrasing.
- One fact per line.
- Each line must be a short, self-contained sentence.
- Each line must start with "The user".
- Do not guess or infer missing information.
- If no facts exist, reply "{0}".
